About Strand After Dark

Strand After Dark is an independent guide to adult nightlife along South Carolina's Grand Strand. We keep a simple, up-to-date list of the area's gentlemen's clubs so visitors can see what's open, where it is, and what to expect before heading out.

We aren't owned by or affiliated with any of the clubs we list, and we don't sell tickets or bottle service. Listings are based on public information and are reviewed regularly, but hours and policies change often — please confirm directly with the venue before you go.

Spot something out of date, or run a business you'd like us to take a look at? We'd like to hear from you.
