Myrtle Beach Gentlemen's Clubs

The Grand Strand once had more than a dozen clubs. Today it's a tighter list — which makes picking the right one easier.

#1 Pick
North Myrtle Beach

Thee DollHouse

The Strand's longest-running name and its biggest production — concert-style lighting and sound, a full bar, and the latest hours in town. Group reservations available.

  • 3001 Hwy 17 S, North Myrtle Beach, SC 29582
  • Daily 4 PM – 4 AM · Happy hour 4–8 PM
  • (843) 272-3329

#2
Central Myrtle Beach

Masters Gentleman's Club

The largest club in Myrtle Beach, with a big dancer roster seven nights a week, private VIP areas, valet, and a food menu. Contact their concierge ahead for free admission and shuttle service.

  • 1901 Mr Joe White Ave, Ste M, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577
  • Daily 1 PM – 2 AM
  • (843) 916-0972

#3
Seaboard Street

Lust Gentlemen's Club & Steakhouse

Myrtle Beach's newest club leans upscale — a Miami/Vegas feel, jacuzzi suites, dozens of VIP areas, and a real kitchen serving steaks and smoked BBQ until last call.

  • 804 Seaboard St, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577
  • Nightly until 4 AM
  • (843) 839-5878

Getting There
Club Crawl & Rides

Don't Drive the Strand

Seaboard Street has two clubs within a short walk. For North Myrtle Beach, ask Masters or Thee DollHouse about their shuttle, or book a limo or rideshare — Hwy 17 is no place to drive after a night out.

  • Seaboard St: Lust + Treasure Club
  • Shuttles: ask the club concierge

Before You Go

Myrtle Beach Club Tips

Bring a Real ID

Every club is 21+ and checks government-issued ID at the door. No exceptions for the birthday boy.

Call Ahead for Groups

Bachelor parties get better tables, free cover deals, and shuttle pickups by booking a day or two early.

Carry Cash

Most clubs have ATMs, but the fees add up. Small bills go further on stage.

Late Night = North

Want to stay out past 2 AM? Lust and Thee DollHouse run until 4 AM; most others close at 2.
